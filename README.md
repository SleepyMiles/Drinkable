# Drinkable

Drink the drinks you find. The vanilla **Red soda** can now be drunk, and there are **19 new drinks**: 9 sodas (Bolt Cola, Peach Soda, Sparkle Soda, ACE Soda, Vanilla Soda, Cheesy Soda, Climate Soda, Kosher Soda and Mystery Soda) and 10 more (Hush Soda, Craft Beer, Belch Cola, Rocket Fizz, Lucky Lemonade, Hunch Soda, Buddy Spritz, Focus Fizz, Party Pop and Ghost Pepper Pop), each with its own label and buff. Find them as scrap, buy them in the store, or get a free one each day from the **Soda Machine** and the **Drink Machine** (and buy more there, most at half price).

> **Made to go with [FrailtyPlus](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/FrailtyPlus/), [FrailtyMedical](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/FrailtyMedical/), [TZPPlus](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/TZPPlus/) and [EladsHUD+](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/EladsHUDPlus/), but needs none of them.** EladsHUD+ shows every buff and debuff under your health bar with the `time left`, and you can point at an icon to see what it does. Without it, the same icons with their time left show in the bottom-left corner of the vanilla HUD, in the same style and spot as TZPPlus' and FrailtyPlus' icons (on the same line, right after theirs).

<details>
<summary>🛒 Store and empty cans</summary>

- Every drink can be bought in the terminal store (`buy red soda`, `buy bolt cola`, `buy craft beer`, ...). Bought ones are tools: worth nothing, gone after use, they scan like a tool, don't count as collected scrap, and (like other tools) aren't lost when everyone dies (also with AdvancedCompany).
- Drinks are pricey in the store ($30 to $150; Belch Cola $60, Focus Fizz $60, Buddy Spritz $70, Craft Beer $80, Rocket Fizz $80, Party Pop $80, Hunch Soda $100, Hush Soda $120, Ghost Pepper Pop $120, Lucky Lemonade $150), and the store's daily sales take **at most 20% off** them. The Soda Machine is the cheap way to get them.
- Scrap drinks you find become an **empty can / bottle** after use. It still sells for its full value (`Empty value %`) and scans as "Empty ...".
- Empty cans are synced to everyone and saved with the ship.
- In the store, the drinks have their own **DRINKS** section (the vanilla store page without any other mod, [TerminalFormatter](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/mrov/TerminalFormatter/), and the AdvancedCompany mobile terminal). With FrailtyMedical, its **MEDICAL** section comes first and DRINKS after it. Option: keep the drinks in the normal item list.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🏪 Soda Machine (ship upgrade)</summary>

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SleepyMiles/Drinkable/main/images/machines.png" width="560">

*The Soda Machine (left) and the Drink Machine (right).*

- Buy the **Soda Machine** in the store's ship upgrades (`buy soda machine`, $500).
  - Its lamp blinks **green** when you can have it, **red** when you can't (not enough credits, or that drink is turned off).
  - The little screen shows **FREE** while you still have your free drink today. Pick a drink and it shows its name and price. After your free drink it stays blank until you pick one.
- Everyone gets **1 free drink per day**, and it's **random** (any button), with the same odds as finding drinks in the facility: the pricier the drink, the rarer it is.
- After that you can **buy the drink you pick at 50% of the store price**, paid with the crew's credits. Machine drinks are tools, like bought ones.
- **Limited stock**: each drink can be bought only 2 to 5 times a day (random, new every day), then it's **SOLD OUT** until tomorrow. **Climate, Cheesy and Kosher Soda, Lucky Lemonade, Hunch Soda and Ghost Pepper Pop** are special (high value): 0 to 3 a day, and the more, the rarer (none 46%, 1: 28%, 2: 17%, 3: 10%). Free drinks don't count. (Can be turned off; there's also an option to show how many are left.) They have their own machine price instead of half the store price: $80 / $90 / $100 for Climate / Cheesy / Kosher Soda, and $100 / $70 / $90 for Lucky Lemonade / Hunch Soda / Ghost Pepper Pop (all settings).
- The first time, it puts itself against the wall between the light switch and the file cabinet (or another free wall). Move it with **[B]** like other furniture.
- Its own retro machine: a lit "Company Soda" sign, a 1940s-style "Drink up! Stay fresh" poster, a label button for each soda, and a drop flap that tips open when your drink comes out. It hums, clunks and dings with the game's own sounds.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🍻 Drink Machine (ship upgrade, new)</summary>

- Buy the **Drink Machine** in the store's ship upgrades (`buy drink machine`, $500). It's the Soda Machine's blue sibling: a lit "Company After Hours" sign and a "Clock out! Kick back" poster.
- It has the drinks the Soda Machine doesn't: **Hush Soda, Craft Beer, Belch Cola, Rocket Fizz, Lucky Lemonade, Hunch Soda, Buddy Spritz, Focus Fizz, Party Pop, Ghost Pepper Pop**.
- Same rules as the Soda Machine (free random drink, then buy the one you pick at 50%, limited stock). The free drink is **1 per player per day from each machine**, so with both you get two.
- The first time, it finds its own free wall (not the Soda Machine's spot). Move it with **[B]**.

</details>

## Configuration

`BepInEx/config/Miles.Drinkable.cfg`, or in-game with **LethalConfig**. Everything except `Display` is **synced from the host**. Your old settings (from older versions and from `Miles.DrinkableSoda.cfg`) are moved over once.

| Section | What |
|---|---|
| General | store on / off, store sale max %, empty value %, Hydration, drinking noise, Speed Balance (only the fastest speed buff counts; off by default), found as scrap or not, how often |
| Drinkable | turn each drink on / off |
| Store price | price of each drink |
| Machine | both machines: sold or not and price of each, free drinks per player per day (each machine), rarity (free drink), sell drinks after the free one, drink price %, limited stock (min / max per day, special drinks max and odds, show stock left), machine prices of the high value drinks (Climate / Cheesy / Kosher Soda, Lucky Lemonade / Hunch Soda / Ghost Pepper Pop) |
| Sodas Buff 1 | Red soda and Bolt Cola (Sugar Rush, Sugar Crash), Peach Soda (Sugar Mend), Sparkle Soda (Brawn), Mystery Soda (its buffs, Cure All, Hyponatremia) |
| Sodas Buff 2 | ACE Soda (Sharp Sight), Vanilla Soda (Exquisite), Cheesy Soda (Cheese Sense), Climate Soda (Adapted), Kosher Soda (Equilibrium, Adrenaline tiers, Equivalence, Dehydration) |
| Sodas Buff 3 | Hush Soda (Silent), Rocket Fizz (Fizz Jump), Lucky Lemonade (Lucky, Forest Giant stun), Belch Cola (Big Burp), Craft Beer (Cold One, Tipsy / Drunk, Wobbly / Staggering) |
| Sodas Buff 4 | Hunch Soda (Danger Sense), Buddy Spritz (Crew Sense, Chill, We'll Make It), Focus Fizz (Locked In), Party Pop (Party), Ghost Pepper Pop (Hot Stuff, Burn Through) |
| Display | your own settings: burp key (Belch Cola), store DRINKS section, bar colors (stamina: EladsHUD+ or the game's meter; health: EladsHUD+), buff icons without EladsHUD+ (on / off, position, size), compass at the top / offset while Cheese Sense or Crew Sense is on |

## Compatibility

- **[FrailtyPlus](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/FrailtyPlus/)** 1.2.0+ (optional): Cure All heals all its injuries; during Equilibrium its injuries give Equivalence, which halves them. Without it, Equivalence only comes from the infection.
- **[TZPPlus](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/TZPPlus/)**: Mystery Soda's Cure All clears the vanilla TZP effects, not Lethal Dose / Withdrawal.
- **[FrailtyMedical](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/FrailtyMedical/)**: its MEDICAL store section comes first, then DRINKS. With its "no healing while bleeding" rule, Sugar Mend doesn't heal while you bleed, but Equivalence does.
- **[EladsHUD+](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/EladsHUDPlus/)** (optional): buff and debuff icons with time left and tooltips under your health bar. Without it, the icons and time left show in the bottom-left corner like TZPPlus' and FrailtyPlus' icons (same frame, time above each, a little smaller; more lines when you have many). Its stamina bar takes the color of your stamina / run speed buff and its health bar the healing buff's (Sugar Mend pink, Equivalence cyan); without it, the game's own stamina meter takes the color.
- **LethalLib**: needed for the Soda Machine and the Drink Machine (everything else works without it).
- **TerminalFormatter**: the DRINKS section shows in its store table too.
- **AdvancedCompany** (SleepyMiles V81 version): the DRINKS section shows in the mobile terminal store too.

## Play them together

These mods are made by **SleepyMiles** to work as one set. They share one row of buff / debuff icons (or EladsHUD+'s status row) and the **DRINKS** / **MEDICAL** store sections, and they know about each other's effects. Each one also works on its own (FrailtyMedical needs FrailtyPlus).

| Mod | What it adds |
|---|---|
| [TZPPlus](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/TZPPlus/) | The TZP heals you, shows its gas, builds up damage reduction (fall damage too). Too much gives a Lethal Dose. |
| [FrailtyPlus](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/FrailtyPlus/) | Injuries: broken / fractured legs and arms, bleeding, concussions, Paralyze. A rebuilt Frailty. |
| [FrailtyMedical](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/FrailtyMedical/) | *Beta.* Bandage, First Aid Kit, Morphine (revive a teammate), Epinephrine, Antifungal, Steroids, Painkillers, Antibiotics and Sedatives to treat them. |
| **Drinkable** (this mod) | Drink the Red soda, 19 new drinks with their own buffs, the Soda Machine and the Drink Machine. |
| [EladsHUD+](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/SleepyMiles/EladsHUDPlus/) | Elad's HUD for V81, with a status row that shows every buff, debuff and injury from all of these with the `time left`. |
